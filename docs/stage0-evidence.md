Four digests are marked `n/a` for Solidity deliberately, and the reason is a
privacy property rather than an optimisation: `nullifier`, `bidCommitment`,
`subjectCommitment` and `credDigest` arrive at the contract as Groth16 **public
signals**, so the contract compares them instead of recomputing them. The
contract therefore never needs an arity-6 Poseidon and never touches a
credential field.

## Measured on-chain Poseidon gas

Plan Section 13.1 warned this is the most underestimated task in the build.
Measured on the local EVM, optimizer `runs: 1`, evm target `paris`:

| Operation | Gas |
|---|---|
| `hash2` (Poseidon arity 2, Merkle parent) | 34,096 |
| `bidLeaf` (Poseidon arity 5) | 254,514 |
| **Implied cost of one leaf append** (1 leaf + 5 parents) | **~425,000** |

Add Groth16 verification and a bid submission is roughly 700k gas. On a
permissioned free-gas chain with a 100M block gas limit this is comfortable,
but it is why the genesis config sets a high `gasLimit` and a zero gas price.

### Why the leaf stays arity 5

An arity-5 Poseidon costs 7.5x an arity-2 on-chain, so `bidLeaf` could be
built from four chained `hash2` calls for ~136k instead of 254k. We keep
arity 5 because the binding constraint is **circuit** size, not gas: in-circuit,
one Poseidon(5) is roughly 240 constraints against roughly 960 for four
chained Poseidon(2). Gas is free on this chain; browser proving time is not.
Recorded here so the trade-off is not revisited by accident.

## Contract size

Both Poseidon libraries fit under the EIP-170 limit of 24,576 bytes, so no
raised contract-size limit is required and the contracts are deployable on a
standard EVM:

| Library | Deployed bytes |
|---|---|
| `PoseidonT3` (arity 2) | 16,859 |
| `PoseidonT6` (arity 5) | 20,612 |
| `EncodingHarness` (test only) | 1,594 |

This needed `optimizer.runs = 1` with `viaIR` **off**. With `viaIR: true` and
`runs: 200`, `PoseidonT6` compiled to 121,884 bytes - five times the limit.
Do not change those compiler settings without re-checking sizes.

Poseidon is deployed once and linked by every contract that needs it, rather
than inlined per contract.
